The publicly available ERAA 2025 dataset can be downloaded from the ENTSO-E website here: https://www.entsoe.eu/eraa/2025/modelling-data/#Input%20Data
Note: Newer releases of the dataset may require minor adjustments ("debugging") to the file structures used in the Jupyter Notebook to ensure compatibility with the downloaded dataset.
The required folder structure of the downloaded dataset can be seen in "ERAA_Dataset_Folder_Structure.pdf"
